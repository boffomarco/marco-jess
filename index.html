Two Worlds, One Love

From Peterborough to Ca'Rainati

❦

A love story that stretches across the Atlantic Ocean, uniting the serene lakes and forests of Ontario, Canada with the historic rolling hills and vineyards of Veneto, Italy.

Though raised over 6,500 kilometres apart, fate had a shared chapter in store for Jess and Marco. Blending warm Canadian hospitality with vibrant Italian passion, they discovered that home is not a single place on a map — but wherever they are together.

On September 4, 2027, in the foothills of Monte Grappa, Marco and Jess will exchange vows before their cherished family and friends who have journeyed from near and far.

The Settings

Ceremony & Reception Venues

❦

Our wedding celebration brings together two breathtaking historical gems of the Veneto region.

Ceremony Venue

Santuario della Madonna del Monte

Address: Colle Castellaro / Via Sopracastello, 31020 San Zenone degli Ezzelini (Treviso), Italy

Affectionately known as Chiesetta Rossa (the Little Red Church) for its distinguished red masonry, this historic 19th-century sanctuary stands on the ancient medieval foundations of the Ezzelini castle.

Perched proudly atop Colle Castellaro, the church offers an extraordinary panoramic vantage point embracing the Venetian plain on one side and the dramatic peaks of Monte Grappa on the other.

🚗
Parking & Access: Dedicated guest parking at the foot of the hill with a brief scenic walkway to the sanctuary. Shuttle service from downtown Bassano del Grappa will also be available.
👠
Footwear Tip: The hill paths and terrace feature cobblestones and natural stone; block heels or dress flats are warmly recommended.

Reception Venue

Villa Ca' Rezzonico

Address: Via Cà Rezzonico, 36061 Bassano del Grappa (Vicenza), Italy

A crown jewel of neoclassical architecture, Villa Ca' Rezzonico was designed in the 18th century by celebrated architect Baldassarre Longhena for the noble family of Pope Clement XIII.

Framed by magnificent statues by Orazio Marinali, historic frescoes by Antonio Canova, and lush Italian gardens, this aristocratic estate is located just ~15 minutes from the ceremony.

🚗
Parking: Convenient private on-site parking directly inside the villa estate gates.
🥂
Settings: Air-conditioned frescoed dining halls, picturesque porticos, and illuminated night-time garden terraces.

Welcome to Veneto

Travel & Accommodation

❦

We are thrilled to welcome ~150 guests, including many traveling overseas from Canada! Here is essential guidance on arriving and enjoying your stay in our corner of northern Italy.

✈️ Recommended Airports

🛬

Venice Marco Polo (VCE)

~50–60 minutes drive to San Zenone

The primary international gateway for guests traveling from Canada. Major airlines offer direct transatlantic connections from Toronto (YYZ) and Montréal (YUL) in summer, or easy one-stop connections through European hubs (Frankfurt, Paris, London, Amsterdam).

🛫

Treviso Airport (TSF)

~40–45 minutes drive to San Zenone

A convenient regional airport (Antonio Canova) primarily serving European carriers such as Ryanair and Wizz Air. Ideal for guests extending their European holiday or connecting from the UK, Ireland, or central Europe.

🏡 Nearby Towns to Stay

San Zenone degli Ezzelini

Ceremony Location

Stay right where the wedding ceremony takes place! Cozy bed & breakfasts, holiday homes, and rustic agriturismi nestled among peaceful olive groves and rolling hills.

Asolo

~10 min drive

Known as "The City of a Hundred Horizons". A romantic medieval hilltop village famous for aristocratic villas, fine dining, charming cafes, and luxury boutique hotels.

Bassano del Grappa

Reception Venue Town

Home to our banquet and evening celebration at Villa Ca' Rezzonico! A lively historic town along the Brenta River, renowned for the wooden Palladian bridge (Ponte Vecchio), grappa distilleries, and vibrant piazzas.

Castelfranco Veneto

~20 min drive

A walled medieval town celebrated as the birthplace of Renaissance master Giorgione. Offers excellent railway connections to Venice, Padua, and Treviso.

🚗 Local Transport & Practical Tips

🚘

Rental Cars

Renting a car at Venice or Treviso airport is the most flexible way to explore the foothills, wine routes, and charming towns. International drivers need a valid home driver's license and an International Driving Permit (IDP).

🚆

Trains (Trenitalia)

Fast and dependable regional trains connect Venice Santa Lucia / Mestre with Castelfranco Veneto and Bassano del Grappa. Train tickets can be purchased on the official Trenitalia website or app.

🚌

Wedding Shuttle

We will arrange transport from downtown Bassano del Grappa to the ceremony at Chiesetta Rossa on the morning of September 4, and from the ceremony to the reception. More details will follow closer to the date.

Got Questions?

Questions & Answers

❦

What is the dress code?

Formal Italian Summer Attire. Suits or sharp blazers for gentlemen; elegant cocktail dresses, floor-length gowns, or chic dressy separates for ladies. Please consider the sunny climate and historic stone walkways when selecting footwear (chunky heels or dressy flats are recommended).

What will the weather be like in early September?

Early September in the Veneto foothills is gorgeous and pleasant. Daytime temperatures usually range between 24°C and 28°C (75°F–82°F) with plenty of sunshine, cooling slightly in the evening. We suggest packing a light wrap or blazer for nighttime.

What are the travel entry requirements for Canadian guests?

Canadian passport holders must ensure their passport is valid for at least 3 months beyond the planned departure date from the Schengen zone. Check before traveling for any ETIAS European travel authorization updates applicable in 2027.

Can I bring a plus-one or children?

Due to venue capacity, our invitations are specifically addressed to designated guests. Please refer to your personal invitation or contact us directly if you have any questions regarding your party.

Will dietary restrictions or allergies be accommodated?

Absolutely! Please specify any vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or specific food allergy requirements in the RSVP Google Form so our caterers can prepare a wonderful custom meal for you.

Is there transport from the ceremony to the reception?

Yes! We will arrange a shuttle from downtown Bassano del Grappa to the Chiesetta Rossa for the ceremony, and then from the ceremony to Villa Ca' Rezzonico for the reception. More logistical details will be included with your formal invitation.
