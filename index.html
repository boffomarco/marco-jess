Ceremony & Reception Venues

❦

A hilltop chapel for our vows, followed by a villa celebration in Bassano del Grappa.

Ceremony Venue

Santuario della Madonna del Monte

Address: Colle Castellaro / Via Sopracastello, 31020 San Zenone degli Ezzelini (Treviso), Italy

This hilltop 19th-century chapel, known as Chiesetta Rossa, overlooks the Veneto plain and Monte Grappa.

🚗
Arrival: Parking is at the foot of the hill, followed by a short walk. A shuttle will run from Bassano del Grappa.
👠
Footwear: The paths are cobbled; block heels or flats are best.

Address: Via Cà Rezzonico, 36061 Bassano del Grappa (Vicenza), Italy

Villa Ca' Rezzonico pairs neoclassical architecture and frescoed halls with peaceful Italian gardens, around 15 minutes from the ceremony.

Fly into Venice or Treviso, stay in a nearby town, and explore Veneto at your own pace.

🛬

Venice Marco Polo (VCE)

🛫

~40–45 minutes drive to San Zenone

A convenient regional airport served by European carriers, including Ryanair and Wizz Air.

Got Questions?

Questions & Answers

❦
What is the dress code?

Formal Italian Summer Attire. Suits or sharp blazers for gentlemen; elegant cocktail dresses, floor-length gowns, or chic dressy separates for ladies. Please consider the sunny climate and historic stone walkways when selecting footwear (chunky heels or dressy flats are recommended).

What will the weather be like in early September?

Early September in the Veneto foothills is gorgeous and pleasant. Daytime temperatures usually range between 24°C and 28°C (75°F–82°F) with plenty of sunshine, cooling slightly in the evening. We suggest packing a light wrap or blazer for nighttime.

What are the travel entry requirements for Canadian guests?

Canadian passport holders must ensure their passport is valid for at least 3 months beyond the planned departure date from the Schengen zone. Check before traveling for any ETIAS European travel authorization updates applicable in 2027.

Can I bring a plus-one or children?

Due to venue capacity, our invitations are specifically addressed to designated guests. Please refer to your personal invitation or contact us directly if you have any questions regarding your party.

Will dietary restrictions or allergies be accommodated?

Absolutely! Please specify any vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or specific food allergy requirements in the RSVP Google Form so our caterers can prepare a wonderful custom meal for you.

Is there transport from the ceremony to the reception?

Yes! We will arrange a shuttle from downtown Bassano del Grappa to the Chiesetta Rossa for the ceremony, and then from the ceremony to Villa Ca' Rezzonico for the reception. More logistical details will be included with your formal invitation.
